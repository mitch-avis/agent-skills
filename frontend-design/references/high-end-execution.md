# High-end execution

Use this when the brief calls for a more premium, distinctive, or launch-grade result than the
default frontend flow would normally produce.

## Zero-tolerance defaults

Avoid these unless the brief clearly calls for them:

- generic display fonts with no product character
- stock card grids carrying the whole page
- safe purple-tech gradients standing in for direction
- motion that consists only of small hover lifts and identical fade-ups

## Variance engine

When the brief permits stronger design moves, choose both a visual world and a layout world.

### Visual world

- Ethereal glass: dark or luminous, layered light, restrained translucency
- Editorial luxury: tactile surfaces, typography-led hierarchy, richer material cues
- Soft structuralism: quiet backgrounds, strong composition, large typographic contrast

### Layout world

- Asymmetrical bento: grouped tiles with hierarchy rather than uniform cards
- Z-axis cascade: overlapping layers that create controlled depth
- Editorial split: a strong content axis paired with a contrasting visual axis

Commit to one of each instead of mixing everything at once.

## Premium component patterns

- Nested shells: give major surfaces a clear outer container and inner content layer when the world
  supports it
- Button-with-core: a CTA can carry an internal icon chip or secondary motion target without
  becoming noisy
- Macro whitespace: premium pages usually need more breathing room than default app layouts

## Motion choreography

- One authored reveal matters more than motion on every element
- Reserve directional slides for actual spatial navigation
- Use spring or carefully tuned easing for tactile actions when the stack supports it
- Keep the page readable before animation completes

## Finish check

- The first viewport expresses a clear thesis, not just a mood
- The layout has one memorable decision and enough restraint around it
- The motion clarifies hierarchy, not just polish
- The assets or proof carry the product story, not decorative chrome
